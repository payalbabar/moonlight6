## 📊 Feedback Summary

A total of **50 Preprod users** (students, developers, Web3 users, and others) tested **StegoVault** on Midnight Preprod and submitted structured validation feedback.

### Key Insights & Supported Themes:

1. **High Overall Satisfaction**: All 50 participants gave 5/5 ratings. The core MVP flow — encrypt → steganographically hide → on-chain seal → recover — was validated as intuitive.
2. **UI/UX Polish**: Users noted the cyberpunk aesthetic was unique and engaging. Several suggested making the payload capacity bar more prominent.
3. **Onboarding**: Users appreciated the guided walkthrough modal. A few suggested even more step-by-step wallet setup guidance.
4. **Privacy & Security**: The AES-256-GCM + LSB steganography combination received strong positive reactions for its privacy-first design.
5. **Feature Requests**: Users expressed interest in multi-image vaults, mobile support improvements, and shareable encrypted vaults.
